Methodology & how this stays current

  • This is a live feed, not a fixed snapshot. A daily job checks every state and federal source against its official page and recent news, and separately scans nationally for district stories that aren't tracked yet. High-confidence findings (an official source, or independent corroboration) are published directly to the data behind this page; lower-confidence findings wait in "Needs review" above.
  • What counts as high confidence: a primary source (a .gov page, an official agency/board/mayor's-office announcement) or the same fact reported independently by 2+ reputable outlets. A single news outlet with no official confirmation is held for review rather than published automatically.
  • District coverage grows as it's discovered. The seed set below started as 7 bellwether districts; the daily national scan adds high-confidence new districts directly, and holds lower-confidence ones for review. It will never be a full census of ~13,000 U.S. districts — large or well-covered districts are what a national news scan can realistically catch.
  • "No action identified" is not the same as "no policy exists." A state may have district-level or informal guidance not yet captured here; treat blank states as gaps to verify, not confirmed negatives.
